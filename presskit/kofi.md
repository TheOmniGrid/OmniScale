# OmniScale

One Windows app for the upscaler runtimes a game ships with -- swap DLSS,
FSR, and XeSS versions, manage OptiScaler and DLSS Enabler, and get the
truth about what FSR 4 actually needs. One person builds it; the people
who use it keep it going.

OmniScale isn't on any store. It's donationware, and Ko-fi is one of the
two ways to get a build -- a one-time coffee if that's what you'd rather
do, no subscription required.

## Buy a coffee

Whatever you'd normally spend on this counts. There's no minimum that
means more than another -- support here goes straight toward tracking new
runtime versions as vendors ship them, keeping the app working across
Windows and driver updates, and answering the people who report what's
broken.
