# OmniScale

**Perfect scale. Every game.**

## The hook

One Windows app for the upscaler runtimes a game ships with: see what's in
each game, swap it for a different version, and put the original back --
per game, with backups and a record of every write. Built by one person,
kept alive by the people who use it.

## What OmniScale is

OmniScale swaps DLSS, DLSS Frame Generation, DLSS Ray Reconstruction, AMD
FSR 3.1, and Intel XeSS runtimes, with an Authenticode signature check
before every write and a one-click revert. It manages OptiScaler and DLSS
Enabler through a shared shim slot so the two can't collide, updates whole
runtime sets -- NVIDIA Streamline, AMD's FidelityFX SDK 2, DirectStorage,
the Agility SDK -- all-or-nothing, and finds games across Steam, GOG, Epic,
Ubisoft Connect, Xbox, Battle.net and EA automatically.

It's free software (GPL-3.0, since it's a modified version of DLSS Swapper,
https://github.com/beeradmoore/dlss-swapper) and donationware: it isn't on
any store, and the only way to get a build is directly from this page or
from Ko-fi (KOFI_LINK).

OmniScale is honest about its limits, too: it does not and cannot switch
on FSR 4 by copying files -- that's a driver feature, and OmniScale reports
what's present and leaves the verdict to your hardware and driver instead
of claiming one. That statement is still true after you become a patron --
this page won't tell you otherwise.

## What you are not paying for

Exclusivity. OmniScale is GPL-3.0: everyone who receives a build gets the
complete source alongside it, and every freedom that license grants -- to
use it, study it, modify it, and share it. Nothing in these tiers changes
that, and nothing here is licensed differently to a top-tier patron than
to someone who asked for a free copy because they couldn't afford one.
What tiers buy is time, priority, and a say in what happens next -- not a
better license, and not a version anyone else is locked out of.
